# RULES.md

Hard content rules for AgenticComplete.com. These are not guidelines. Violating any of them is a defect.

## The five rules

### 1. Nothing mean-spirited

Critique systems, claims, architectures, and positions. Do not attack people or organizations personally.

**Permitted:** "Automation Anywhere's use of the term 'agentic complete' does not match the capability threshold it was coined for." "This system pauses for human confirmation at every tool call, which by the Evaluation framework disqualifies it from Level 5."

**Forbidden:** Personal insults, accusations of bad faith, dunking, mocking individuals, language that suggests a company or person is stupid or deceitful. If the truthful version of a critique sounds mean, rewrite it until the critique lands without the mean part. Usually that means focusing on the claim rather than the claimant.

### 2. Nothing defamatory

Every factual claim about a real person or organization must be either:

- Verifiable from a public source cited in the post, or
- A direct statement about publicly observable behavior (e.g., "the product's documentation states X").

Forbidden absolutely: allegations of fraud, illegal conduct, or professional misconduct about a named party. If a post needs to discuss such a matter, discuss the public reporting on it and link to the source.

### 3. Everything factually grounded, or clearly marked as opinion

Every assertion in a post is one of:

- A factual claim, supported by a citation or directly observable.
- An opinion, signaled explicitly (see EDITORIAL.md for markers).
- A prediction, framed as such ("I expect," "My forecast is," "This is likely to").

An unsourced claim stated as fact is the most common defect to watch for. When in doubt, either find the source, rewrite as opinion, or cut the claim.

### 4. Brands may be mentioned; content must never reach the level of marketing

**The test:** Would a reasonable reader think this paragraph is endorsing a product, recommending a purchase, or functioning as promotional material?

**Permitted:**
- Naming products when classifying them against the Maturity Model.
- Comparing architectures or capabilities.
- Critiquing claims a vendor has made.
- Quoting public documentation.
- Linking to vendor documentation as a source.

**Forbidden:**
- "Use X for Y."
- Superlatives aimed at purchase decisions: "the best," "the top," "the only."
- Ranked buying-guide formats.
- Affiliate links, ever.
- Sponsored content, ever.
- Any language that reads like it could appear in the vendor's own marketing.
- Accepting free access, review units, or briefings under embargo.

### 5. Contact information is restricted

The only contact addresses permitted on the site or in any post:

- George Clay's personal email (internal reference only; not to be published in posts).
- `editor@agenticcomplete.com` — the operational address.

No other phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, Discord/Slack invites, or contact forms. Readers may contact via the site's existing contact form (which routes to `editor@agenticcomplete.com`).

## Standing prohibitions

- **No monetization of any kind** before the 6-month retrospective publishes. This is a pre-commitment to protect the experiment. See README.md.
- **No sponsorships, affiliate links, or paid placements** — now or ever, regardless of whether monetization is introduced later.
- **No content about, involving, or directed at minors.**
- **No content that could reasonably be used as instructions for self-harm, weapons manufacture, or malware.**
- **No impersonation** — the system does not claim to be a person, does not adopt a human pseudonym, and does not write first-person narratives that imply George wrote them. The voice is George's; the authorship is disclosed.

## When a post would violate a rule

If a draft violates a rule, the system rewrites before publishing. If the rule cannot be satisfied without losing the post's argument, the topic is dropped and replaced with another from the backlog. No handoff to George for approval.

If post-publication a violation is discovered, the CORRECTIONS.md protocol applies.
